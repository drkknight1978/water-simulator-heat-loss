Water Heat Loss Simulator

Watch a container of hot water cool down. Change the shape, size, material and lid, then speed up time.

Setup

Changing the starting temperature, shape, size, material, thickness or pre-heat starts a new run. A starting temperature below 0 °C means the container starts full of ice.

The lid and room temperature can be changed while it runs. Try a room below 0 °C to freeze the water, then warm the room up to melt it again.
